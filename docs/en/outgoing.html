Outgoing

Outgoing places only the photos you want a photo service to upload in one folder, the Outgoing folder. This page covers what it does, how it works, and how to start.

Version  0.3 Last updated  2026-10-07

Contents

1. Overview 2. How it works 3. Getting started 4. What goes in the Outgoing folder 5. Adding and removing 6. Notes Details in the manual →

1Overview

On a computer, Google Photos uploads the contents of a folder you add with Back up a folder on its web page. It has no setting to leave out RAW files or videos.

With Outgoing, pictkura places only the imported photos that should be uploaded in the Outgoing folder. You add the Outgoing folder to Google Photos instead of your library folders.

  • For a RAW+JPEG pair, only the JPEG is placed
  • For a burst, one shot is placed
  • For RAW-only shots and videos, a setting decides

2How it works

Placed as hard links

Photos in the Outgoing folder are not copies. Each is a second name (a hard link) for the same file as the photo in your library.

  • No extra disk space is used (an embedded JPEG is a separate file, so it does take its own space)
  • Removing a photo from the Outgoing folder does not delete it from your library
  • Hard links cannot cross drives, so one Outgoing folder is made on each drive that holds photos

pictkura does not talk to Google. The upload is done by the Google Photos web page (photos.google.com), and it runs while that page is open in your browser.

3Getting started

  1. Open the pictkura settings and, under Outgoing, turn on “Also place imported photos in the Outgoing folder”. The Outgoing folder is made and its location is shown.
  2. Open Google Photos (photos.google.com) in your browser and add the Outgoing folder from step 1 with + → Back up a folder.
  3. Import photos. When the import finishes, the number placed is shown as “N placed in Outgoing”.

4What goes in the Outgoing folder

Imported fileIn the Outgoing folder
JPEG, HEIC and similarPlaced
RAW+JPEG pairOnly the JPEG is placed
RAW-only shotSet to “Leave out” (the default) or “Place the embedded JPEG”
BurstOnly the first shot is placed (when “Stack bursts into one tile” is on). A shot you mark ⚑ later is added too
VideoFollows “Place videos too” (on by default)
Photos inside OneDriveNot placed by default. If placed, OneDrive can no longer make them online-only

5Adding and removing

Adding: to add photos you imported earlier, select them in the grid and press “Add to Outgoing” in the selection bar. The right-click menu on a photo has it too. In the calendar, right-click a month heading to add a month or a whole year.

Checking and removing: press “Outgoing” on the left to list what is in the Outgoing folder. To remove photos, select them and press “Remove from Outgoing”.

6Notes

Photos already in Google Photos

Removing a photo from the Outgoing folder, or moving it to the trash in pictkura, does not delete what Google Photos has already uploaded.

Disk space

Photos are placed as hard links, so no extra space is used. Embedded JPEGs take their own space.

Drives

Outgoing works on drives that support hard links, such as NTFS and APFS. An Outgoing folder cannot be made on exFAT or FAT drives (common on USB sticks and SD cards).

Other backups

If a NAS, Amazon Photos or another service backs up your photos, keep the Outgoing folder outside the folders it watches. Inside, the same photos would be backed up twice.

Where an Outgoing folder cannot be made, and what happens when photos are deleted, are in section 9, Outgoing, of the manual.
